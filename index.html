Intro 1

"I don't think this is going to work."

Sungchan expected to hear those words the moment he sat down for this stupid blind date, but he hoped to say them first. Like 10 minutes into their meetup, because he was already disinterested prior to coming in, but he had to endure at least an hour after all the tedious and completely unsolicited effort Eunseok put in to set this up. Sigh.

"Yeah, I agree. Maybe we could stay in touch?" Not that Sungchan would, but he had to be polite. "It was nice to meet you—"

"You know, word of advice. Don't lie about yourself if you want to date someone."

Sungchan blinked, confused at the sudden change in atmosphere. "Excuse me?"

The person across from him scoffed and rolled their eyes. "Don't play dumb. You've got some nerve lying to me on a first date. I've never dated anyone before. Drop that crazy purity act, it's just a sorry excuse for being emotionally unavailable."

Okay, ouch. "Well, it is true. This is the first date I've ever been on in my life—"

"With that face and that body? Un-fucking-believable. What kind of 24 year old has never dated? What, are you going to lie to me again and say you never even had sex?"

Sungchan flushed, a mixture of anger and embarrassment bubbling inside him. What was happening? "And what's wrong with being a virgin?" he defensively replied. And immediately regretted it, because that was a damning admission and this stranger did not need further confirmation from him.

"Being a virgin means you've still got the mind of an immature child. You haven't developed the functionalities to be a normal adult. If we dated, then I'd have to handhold you and coddle you, which I don't have the time for. And then I'd have to deal with you being a fucking liar."

Sungchan curled his hands into fists to suppress the anger inside him. He didn't need to be scolded like this, getting told off for being exactly what he was, an inexperienced virgin, sexually and romantically. His friends couldn't care less about it, his family didn't either, not really anyway except for asking twice a year around the holidays if he was seeing anyone, because "It's nice if someone looked after you too, Sungchanie." But they never pressured him into dating someone.

So of course, the one time he tried dating, even at low stakes and low effort, backfired on him terribly. It was a train wreck that he couldn't have even seen coming.

He took a deep shuddering breath, ears burning and blood racing, feeling like he might throw up a little of the coffee he had, which tasted real bland and acrid. Even that should've been a premonition of what was to come.

He could barely hear his own thoughts, but he was still well-aware of the stares of everyone around them who were badly pretending to not listen to their little one-sided deranged argument. Sungchan swallowed and said, as politely and firmly as he could, "Well, good thing this isn't going to work out. Please do not contact me, ever. In this lifetime or the next."

He stood up noisily, chair screeching across the floor as he gathered up his things. The person's eyes bulged, mouth squawking like a bird, "Yah! Where are you going?!"

Sungchan stormed away, head held high despite his desire to run and hide so bad. He wasn't the bad guy in this situation, he didn't say or do anything wrong except be honest. But it was still the shittiest feeling ever.

And it was definitely in his Top 5 Most Humiliating Experiences ever, and Sungchan didn't get embarrassed easily. But to hear all of that on Valentine's Day too, surrounded by a bunch of couples on their own dates—WELL FUCK YOU, SONG EUNSEOK, YOU SICK SON OF A BITCH!!!

Intro 2

Contrary to common perception, Sungchan was not adverse to dating—he just never actively chased it. He was open to the idea, a little intrigued even, but he never went out of his way to seek it.

Sharing his time, space, and efforts into pursuing someone felt cumbersome, when he could be pursuing other things that were more tangible and achievable in life. Like sports trophies, excellent grades, and a lateral career move.

Because if things didn't work out, he knew it was not so much due to his character, but due to his lack of skillset or poorer metric performance, which could be easily improved. Not because he couldn't conform to someone's dating preferences, or handle their emotional mood swinging whims, or reply back ASAP about his whereabouts and deal with the fallout of false cheating allegations which blacklisted him from the dating pool for years (real story of a friend of a friend).

Dating sounded exhausting. High risk, low reward. And Sungchan wasn't much of a gambler.

Oh, and he liked men. Go figure.
